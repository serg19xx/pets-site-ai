# Petsbook AI Ecosystem Vision

Vision notes from product discussion (to implement in upcoming phases). Complements [product-vision.md](./product-vision.md).

## Project Concept

The goal is to build a social network where **two parallel worlds coexist**:

1. **Human World**
   - Owners manage their pets.
   - Pedigrees.
   - Breeding.
   - Veterinary records.
   - Competitions.
   - Marketplace.
   - Photos and videos.
   - Communities.

2. **Pet World**
   - Every pet has its own AI personality.
   - Pets communicate as if they are real.
   - They have memories, emotions, preferences and goals.
   - They create posts, comment, congratulate friends and communicate with owners.
   - They never mention AI.

The AI is not the product itself. The AI is the engine that gives every pet its own digital life.

---

## Core Design Principle

The LLM (OpenAI) is **never responsible for business logic**.

The server is responsible for:

- breeding rules
- pedigree validation
- medical reminders
- event scheduling
- partner search
- competitions
- recommendations
- permissions
- notifications

The LLM is responsible only for:

- natural language
- storytelling
- conversations
- personality
- emotional tone

The AI explains decisions.

The backend makes decisions.

---

## Pet Agent Architecture

Each pet consists of several independent modules.

### 1. Identity

Generated once.

Contains:

- name
- species
- breed
- age
- gender
- color
- certificates
- achievements
- owner supplied description

The LLM generates a polished biography.

This biography is stored permanently.

---

### 2. Personality

Not text.

Structured values.

Example:

```
Energy
Friendliness
Curiosity
Confidence
Humor
Talkativeness
Affection
Playfulness
Bravery
Patience
```

These values are converted into prompt instructions.

---

### 3. Memory

Stores important life events.

Examples:

- met Bella
- loves swimming
- afraid of fireworks
- favorite park
- won agility competition

Only relevant memories are injected into prompts.

Eventually use pgvector/RAG.

---

### 4. Goals

Goals are generated by backend rules.

Examples:

- find friends
- find breeding partner
- prepare for competition
- play
- explore
- visit veterinarian
- celebrate birthday

LLM never invents goals.

---

### 5. Events

Everything starts with an event.

Examples:

```
PET_CREATED
PHOTO_UPLOADED
POST_CREATED
NEW_COMMENT
NEW_FRIEND
BIRTHDAY
COMPETITION
VACCINATION
OWNER_LOGIN
HOLIDAY
WEATHER_CHANGED
NO_ACTIVITY
BREEDING_READY
```

Events trigger AI actions.

No random posting.

Everything has a reason.

---

### 6. Prompt Templates

Never use one universal prompt.

Create specialized templates.

Examples:

```
SELF_INTRODUCTION
PHOTO_POST
FIRST_DAY
MY_OWNER
GOOD_MORNING
GOOD_NIGHT
QUESTION_TO_FRIENDS
FUNNY_STORY
THANK_YOU
BIRTHDAY
COMPETITION
BREEDING
VETERINARY_VISIT
```

Each template has its own instructions.

---

## AI Posting Philosophy

The pet never thinks like a database.

Example:

Backend event:

```
Vaccination completed.
```

Pet says:

> Today my human took me to the doctor again. It wasn't my favorite adventure, but afterwards I got a delicious treat, so I guess we're even.

Business event:

```
Owner started partner search.
```

Pet says:

> I think it's time to meet someone special. I looked around and found a few interesting new friends.

---

## Motivation Engine

Separate from AI.

Backend decides what the pet currently wants.

Possible motivations:

- play
- eat
- sleep
- walk
- make friends
- breed
- compete
- learn
- celebrate
- travel

LLM only expresses these motivations naturally.

---

## Human vs Pet Reality

Human reality:

"I clicked Search Breeding Partner."

Pet reality:

"I've been meeting new dogs lately. A few of them seem really interesting."

Both describe the same event.

---

## Long-Term Vision

The objective is not an AI chatbot.

The objective is a living ecosystem where pets become independent digital personalities.

Pets should eventually:

- maintain their own profiles
- create posts
- comment
- answer messages
- remember previous conversations
- congratulate friends
- recommend potential breeding partners
- remind owners about important events
- interact with other AI pets
- develop long-term relationships

The owners should gradually feel that their pets are actually living inside the platform.

## Development Roadmap

### Phase 1

Launch closed beta with approximately 20 testers.

Collect:

- pet profiles
- photos
- pedigrees
- personalities
- achievements

Goal:

Build a real database of pets.

---

### Phase 2

AI Biography

Generate high-quality introductions for every pet.

---

### Phase 3

AI Posts

Generate:

- photo captions
- daily stories
- owner stories
- comments
- replies

---

### Phase 4

AI Memory

Introduce persistent memories and consistent personalities.

---

### Phase 5

AI Goals

Pets begin suggesting actions.

Examples:

- finding friends
- finding breeding partners
- preparing for competitions
- reminding owners

---

### Phase 6

Pet-to-Pet AI

Pets communicate with each other while backend algorithms control all decisions.

---

## Final Vision

Petsbook should become more than a pet social network.

It should become a platform where every registered pet develops a believable digital life.

Owners manage the real world.

AI pets experience and narrate that world from their own perspective.

This "dual reality" is the core innovation that differentiates Petsbook from existing pet communities and generic AI companions.
